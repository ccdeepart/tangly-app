Common questions

  • I'm stuck and the arrows won't move. The wrong order can lock arrows. Tap Undo or Restart; Hint shows the next good move.
  • When does the daily puzzle change? At midnight, in your phone's time zone.
  • I bought Untangly Plus on another device. Open ⓘ in the app and tap "Restore purchases".
  • Can I move my progress to a new phone? Progress is stored only on your phone. Untangly Plus can always be restored with the same Apple Account.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Untangly does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your solved levels, stars, hints, streak and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Untangly Plus is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account owns Untangly Plus.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates a short text (puzzle number, stars, moves) on your phone and hands it to the share sheet you choose. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

Children

Untangly is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: app.tangly@gmail.com.
